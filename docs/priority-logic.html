Colour by "will this stop me before it resets?"

Every limit gets one question: at my current rate, do I hit 100% before it resets? The session always comes first. Another limit appears on the panel only when it actually threatens you, and the panel colour comes from the worst limit, judged by that question rather than by raw %.

The new rule

RED: it will stop you

The limit is at 100% or at the current rate it runs out before it resets. The panel shows when you'll hit it.

AMBER: cutting it close

At the current rate you'd finish the window at 95–100%. You'll make it, but with little margin.

GREEN: fine

The projected total at reset is below 95%, whatever the raw % says. A week at 64% that ends around 90% is green.

  • Same rule for every limit: session, week, and model-specific weeks like Fable. The windows are independent, so a week reset never touches the session and vice versa.
  • Early in a window elapsed time is floored at 10% of the window, so the projection is cautious rather than missing. A burst of 20% in the first 5 minutes of a session counts as running at a 200% pace, and shows red.
  • Reset passed? The 60 s tick re-checks. A limit whose reset time has gone by is treated as 0%, marked "just reset", and a refresh fires straight away.
  • The "warn at 50 / hot at 80 / colour by" settings go away, replaced by one setting: "amber when projected ≥ 95%".

What the panel shows

  • Session % first, always, followed by the session reset time whenever a session is active.
  • Week or Fable appears only when it is amber or red (or always, if set in Settings), with a letter label and its own colour.
  • Pill tint is the worst colour. If a non-session limit is red, its time (hourglass) replaces the session timer, because that's the one that blocks you.

Rule after stress-testing

RED when the limit is at 100%; or ≤ 2% is left with the reset more than 30 min away; or for the session, the average or last-30-min rate runs out before the reset; or for a weekly limit, it runs out before the reset and (within 24 h or ≥ 90% used).

AMBER when it projects to finish at ≥ 95%, or a weekly run-out isn't urgent yet.

GREEN otherwise. GREY when the data is stale. Model-specific limits tint the pill at most amber.

One decision (row 12): I recommend capping model limits at amber on the pill. The other option is full red, which is right only if you use Fable most of the time. That's the recommendation I'd defend least: if Fable is your daily driver, say "Fable red" and I'll make it a setting.

How pace is calculated: calendar time vs your working time

Today pace counts every hour of the week, nights and weekends included: pace = used × window ÷ elapsed. You work Mon–Fri, so the hours you never use dilute the rate and the week looks safer than it is. The fix is to count only your working hours for weekly limits.

weekly pace = used × work_hours_in_window ÷ work_hours_elapsed

run-out = now + (100 − used) ÷ (used ÷ work_hours_elapsed), counted forward over work hours only, then shown as a clock time

Work hours come from a schedule setting, default Mon–Fri 09:00–19:00. Use outside those hours still counts toward used; it just doesn't stretch the clock. The 5-hour session keeps calendar time, because a session only runs while you're working anyway. Elapsed is still floored at 10% of the window's work hours.

Leave and holidays

A "Day off today" switch was tried and removed: taking one day out of both elapsed and remaining time moved the weekly pace by only ~2 points.

Later: read leave and holidays automatically from the Pace app's DhiHRMS calendar (its my-days endpoint), once that exists.

If you do work late or on weekends

That usage is never lost: it adds to used, which only makes the projection more cautious. If it becomes routine, widen the schedule in Settings (e.g. Mon–Sat, 09–23). There's also a "Calendar time" option for people with no fixed schedule.
